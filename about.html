Who we are

Youth Republic is a nation of youths under the republic of Malawi without political affiliation, working independently towards the development of programs that will help the youth meaningfully contribute to their communities, districts and Nation.

The focus of the republic are Malawian youths aged 13 to 40, aiming to spearhead positive charge, foster personal growth and empower them to be active contributors to their communities. The organization aims to be a platform where the voice of the youth is heard, where they are empowered towards building systems that will aid them in developing their lives economically and instill a sense of patriotism in them.

Mission

equip young with the tools, opportunities, and protection they need to take control of their future-by building resilient systems for skills development, economic participation, and civic engagement that ensure long-term sustainability and generational impact.
